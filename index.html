Why just one watch

Most new watch brands launch with a catalogue — a diver, a dress watch, a field watch, three colours of each. We didn't want to do that. We wanted to spend our first year's budget on one case, one movement, one strap, and get all three exactly right.

That means a solid steel case instead of a plated one, a sapphire crystal that won't scratch on your first Tuesday, and a movement we'd still trust after five years of daily wear. It also means we can tell you exactly what's inside your watch, because it's the only one we make.
